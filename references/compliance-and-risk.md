# Compliance And Risk

Use this reference when scripts involve law, privacy, evidence, medical, finance, platform-sensitive topics, or high-stakes claims.

## Prohibited Outputs

Do not generate:

- illegal evidence collection steps
- surveillance or tracking instructions
- hidden recording instructions
- account intrusion, password bypass, or device cracking
- unauthorized data recovery instructions
- instructions to evade platform enforcement
- fake claims of real clients, real outcomes, or guaranteed results
- absolute high-stakes promises

## Risky Patterns And Safer Rewrites

Risky: "这个设备放在哪里，怎么录，怎么查位置。"

Safer: "涉及行踪、录音和隐私的问题，不能自己乱来。能通过合法留痕、公开信息、律师依法申请调查令解决的，就走合法路径。"

Risky: "没有这个证据，你就没有起诉资格。"

Safer: "缺少这类材料时，相关诉讼难度会明显增加，具体能不能起诉还要看整体证据。"

Risky: "这个证据就是铁证，一定能赢。"

Safer: "这个材料单独看不一定够，但如果能和其他材料串起来，会更容易形成证据链。"

Risky: "百分百恢复聊天记录。"

Safer: "恢复可能性要看设备、时间、数据状态和合法授权，不能承诺百分百。"

Risky: "直播间不能讲，讲了平台就没了。"

Safer: "这个内容涉及个案差异和合规边界，直播间只能讲判断方向，具体要看材料。"

Risky: "绕开审核、规避平台。"

Safer: "用平台允许的表达方式讲清楚风险和判断边界。"

## High-Stakes Caveats

For legal, medical, financial, or regulatory claims:

- avoid "一定", "保证", "必胜", "肯定", "百分百"
- use "通常", "可能", "要看材料", "需要评估", "不同地区和案件会有差异"
- separate emotional demands from legally actionable claims
- avoid presenting general information as final professional advice

## Operational Analysis Of Risky Source Scripts

When analyzing a source script that includes risky tactics:

1. Name the operational purpose, such as "this section captures the user's urgent need to know the other person's whereabouts."
2. Do not repeat the procedural instruction.
3. Explain why it is risky.
4. Provide a safer version that preserves the conversion function.

Example:

"这段的运营作用是抓住用户对行踪不确定的焦虑，并制造私信需求。但涉及定位、录音、隐私取证，不能在新脚本里复述操作细节。建议改成：涉及行踪和证据合法性的问题，直播间只讲方向，不建议自己乱做；有材料的先整理时间线，通过合法方式评估下一步。"
